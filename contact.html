FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

06 TOPICS
01 How far in advance should I book a costume?

We recommend enquiring as early as possible, especially for popular characters, festive seasons, school performances, and group events. Early enquiries give you more time to explore suitable options. All bookings remain subject to costume availability and confirmation.

02 How long can I rent a costume for?

Rental duration depends on the agreed booking terms and the costume selected. Include your event date and expected return date in your enquiry so our team can explain the applicable rental period, collection arrangements, and return deadline before you confirm.

03 Is a deposit required for costume rentals?

A deposit or security payment may be required, depending on the rental arrangement. Please confirm the deposit amount, payment deadline, refund conditions, and any applicable damage or late-return charges with us before paying.

04 Are costumes cleaned before they are rented?

Please contact us to confirm our cleaning and garment-care procedures for your selected costume. Some delicate fabrics, accessories, and decorative pieces may require specialised care. We can also explain any care instructions that apply during your rental.

05 How can I check costume sizes and availability?

Tell us the character or costume you're interested in, whether it is for an adult or child, your preferred size, and the event date. We'll need to check the available options. If you're unsure about sizing, include measurements or ask us for guidance.

06 What if I need to cancel or return a costume late?

Cancellation, rescheduling, and late-return terms depend on the rental agreement. Please contact us as soon as your plans change. Before confirming a booking, ask about cancellation deadlines, refund eligibility, extension options, and any applicable extra charges.
