Need a Little Help?

Have a question about your costume or reservation? Our team is here to help.

Contact Our Team

Each costume is available for a specified rental period. Please check your booking confirmation for the collection date, rental duration and agreed return deadline.

Need your costume for longer? Contact us before your return date to ask about an extension. Extensions are subject to availability and our rental policy.

Check the size guide on the costume product page before placing your reservation. Compare your measurements with the listed chest, waist, hip and length measurements where available.

If you are between sizes or unsure about the fit, contact our team with the costume name and your measurements so we can help you choose.

The rental price, payment deadline and any refundable security deposit will be explained during the booking process.

Before confirming your reservation, review the payment terms, deposit refund conditions and any additional charges stated in your booking details.

Your booking confirmation should include the agreed collection or delivery arrangements, along with the return date and instructions.

Return the costume and its accessories by the agreed deadline. If delivery or courier return is available, confirm the arrangements with our team before booking.

Please handle rented costumes with care and follow the garment-care instructions provided. Do not wash, iron or repair a costume unless our team has approved the method.

If damage occurs, contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation, refund, late-return and damage charges are handled according to the terms provided with your reservation.

A quick reminder: Your confirmed booking terms take priority. Please check your reservation details for the exact rental and return conditions.
