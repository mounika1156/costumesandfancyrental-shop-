SIZE GUIDE

Measure for the Best Fit

Find the perfect fit for every costume with our simple measuring guide. Use a flexible measuring tape and measure comfortably over light clothing.

Before You Begin

Keep the measuring tape straight and snug, but never tight. Stand naturally for accurate measurements.

01

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of the chest, keeping it level under the arms.

02

Waist

Measure around the natural waistline without pulling the tape too tightly.

03

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of the hips while standing with your feet together.

04

Height

Stand straight against a wall and measure from the floor to the top of the head.

05

Inseam

Measure from the top of the inner thigh down to the ankle, especially for trousers and full-length costumes.

Our fitting tip: For children's costumes, check height and chest measurements first. If your child falls between sizes, compare the costume's fit and size chart before making your reservation.

How do I measure myself for a costume?

Use a flexible measuring tape to measure your chest, waist, and hips where relevant. For full-length costumes, also check your height and inseam. Keep the tape level and comfortably snug without pulling it tight. Compare your measurements with the size information for your chosen costume.

How can I check if my size is available to rent?

Visit the costume's product page and check the available size or reservation details. Availability may depend on your rental dates and existing bookings. If the information is not displayed online, contact our team with the costume name, required size, and rental dates so we can help you check.

Can I try on a costume before renting it?

Fitting options depend on our shop's location and rental policy. Contact us before visiting to confirm whether a fitting appointment is available for your chosen costume and size.

What measurements should I check for a children's costume?

Start with your child's height and chest measurements, then check the waist and inseam if the costume includes fitted trousers or full-length clothing. Review any special sizing notes provided for the specific design.
